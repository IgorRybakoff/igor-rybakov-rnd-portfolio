# Human Clarity AI

Human Clarity AI starts from one principle:

> AI should not reduce the quality of human thinking.

## Concept

A cognitive safety layer between a person and an AI system. Its purpose is not to replace thinking or act as a generic coach, but to identify when AI interaction creates excessive delegation, false certainty, bias amplification, or loss of user agency.

## Candidate evaluation dimensions

- cognitive load;
- agency preservation;
- bias amplification;
- uncertainty visibility;
- depth of user participation;
- reversibility of AI-supported decisions.

## Current status

Research concept and evaluation direction. The next stage is to define measurable protocols and compare ordinary AI assistance with a Human Clarity mode in controlled user studies.
